You come home with pictures<br>
of everything but yourself.

There are many ways to be a person,<br>
and this is indeed one of them,<br>
when you are so full of kindling<br>
that you cannot risk a match.<br>
Would you burn what you have built?<br>
Each board cut by hand,<br>
sanded clean, pressure-treated—

Except: the spare planks beg for the bonfire.<br>
Every flame a tongue,<br>
every blaze a congregation,<br>
smoke and fury,<br>
heat and sound,<br>
spark and sweat.

How long is immortality?<br>
How hot is immolation?<br>
In time, your forests will burn,<br>
and you with them,<br>
longing and belonging, <br>
in spite of your choosing,<br>
or in lieu of it,<br>
or in light of it.
